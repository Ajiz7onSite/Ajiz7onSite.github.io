The workflow

Five steps.
One clear goal.

Keep the process focused. Each stage has one job: make the final template more useful, more visual, and easier to customize.

01

Discover the food

Start with the product and its personality. The category, audience, atmosphere, and visual direction become the foundation.

Food identityAudienceDirection
02

Build the visual language

Choose the palette, typography, imagery, shapes, and details that make the template feel connected to its food concept.

ColorTypeImagery
03

Shape the experience

Turn the visual direction into a useful page structure with menus, stories, galleries, reviews, FAQs, and clear actions.

UX flowSectionsCTA
04

Add movement

Layer in scroll reveals, hover states, parallax details, floating elements, and other motion without losing usability.

AnimationInteractionParallax
05

Refine and launch

Polish spacing, responsive behavior, navigation, visual consistency, and the final code so the template is ready to customize.

ResponsiveClean codeDeploy
Design principle

Make the first screen do the talking.

Food websites need appetite before detail. The hero, product imagery, typography, and first action should communicate the concept quickly.

Clear hierarchy

Important content gets visual priority.

Useful motion

Animation supports attention, not distraction.

Real structure

Pages are designed around practical user flows.

Easy editing

Simple files keep customization approachable.
